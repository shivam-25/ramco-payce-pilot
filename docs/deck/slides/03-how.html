THE ONE DESIGN DECISION

Rules in code. Judgement in the model.

Nine stages. Two use a model — outlined in pink. Every box can refuse, and nothing sends itself.

1 · PII gate

regex + managed NER · first, always

2 · Normalise

3 export schemas, one shape · <1ms

3 · Lookup

region, tier, SLA clock · <1ms

4 · Injection screen

patterns + PROMPT_ATTACK · before a model

5 · Classify

Nova Micro · nine queues · 340ms

6 · Router

money or P1 → a person · <1ms

7 · Dedupe

5th report raises an incident · <1ms

8 · Retrieve & answer

Nova Pro · dated, cited · ~1.5s

9 · Output check

C1–C9, or withhold it · <1ms

A model that is 97% right about whether money is involved is 3% wrong about money — so that question never reaches one. Stages 1 and 4 pair deterministic regex with Bedrock Guardrails, because regex is complete over identifiers it can enumerate and blind to names and paraphrases.
