PROOF — WHAT IT DOES WHEN IT DOES NOT KNOW

A pointer is not an answer.

Asked for a rate that is not in the corpus, the first version pointed at the article: cited, honest, and useless. It passed the citation check while telling the customer nothing.

The 24 KB articles have no body in this export — every one says “see Confluence”. So G9 drops those stubs before the draft call rather than catching the pointer after paying for it, and the model is left with nothing to manufacture from.

It now says so plainly, and sends nothing. An escalation carries no draft and no citations — a refusal is not a reply, and shipping its reasoning as one put internal text in the field the agent sends.
